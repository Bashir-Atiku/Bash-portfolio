Flare Gas Recovery Optimization

An engineering optimization project focused on improving flare-gas recovery while maintaining safe and stable operating conditions.

Project Overview:

The project involved studying the relationship between relief-header gas flow, header pressure, recovered gas flow, and assist-steam flow. The goal was to develop a better approach for adjusting operating settings over time while maintaining system constraints.
